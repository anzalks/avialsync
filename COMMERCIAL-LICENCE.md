# Commercial licensing

AvialSync is dual-licensed.

**Option 1 — AGPL-3.0-or-later (default, no cost).** Use, study, modify and
redistribute it freely. The condition is reciprocity: if you convey a modified
version, including letting others use it over a network, you release your
changes under the same licence. Full terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

**Option 2 — a commercial licence.** If your situation cannot accommodate the
AGPL's reciprocity, the copyright holder can grant separate terms.

## When you need the commercial option

Most people do not. Academic use, running it in your lab, publishing papers
about results obtained with it, writing plugins for your own rig, and
contributing improvements back all work under the AGPL and cost nothing.

The commercial licence exists for cases such as:

- shipping AvialSync, or a derivative of it, inside a closed-source product;
- offering it as a hosted or managed service without publishing your changes;
- embedding it in instrumentation you sell, where releasing the surrounding
  source is not possible;
- an organisation whose policy forbids AGPL software outright.

## Plugins

A plugin that only uses the documented `TimeSeriesSource`, `VideoSource` and
`SessionSource` interfaces is your own work, and you choose its licence — you
are not obliged to publish a loader for your lab's proprietary format. What the
AGPL reaches is AvialSync itself and derivatives of it.

If you intend to distribute a closed plugin alongside a modified AvialSync, the
boundary is worth agreeing in writing first; ask.

The commercial option depends on the copyright holder being able to license the
whole work. Contributions are therefore accepted under the terms in
[CLA.md](CLA.md), which lets your contribution ship under both licences while
you keep the copyright in your own work.
